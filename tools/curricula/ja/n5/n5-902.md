# Time, days and dates - 時・曜日・月・日

**Meaning** · telling the time, naming the day of the week, the month and the date

Clock time, months and days of the week are regular: a number plus {f|時|じ}, {f|分|ふん}, {f|月|がつ} or
{f|曜日|ようび}, with a few fixed readings. The days of the month are the part to learn by heart: the first ten
use the old Japanese numbers.

Questions: {f|何時|なんじ} (what time), {f|何曜日|なんようび} (what day), {f|何月|なんがつ}
(what month), {f|何日|なんにち} (what date). A full date goes from big to small:
{f|二〇二六年十月二十七日|にせんにじゅうろくねんじゅうがつにじゅうしちにち}, then the day of the week.

## Examples

<pre>
{f|毎朝|まいあさ}<b>{f|七時|しちじ}</b>に{f|起|お}きます。
I get up at seven every morning.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|会議|かいぎ}は<b>{f|三時半|さんじはん}</b>からです。
The meeting starts at half past three.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|今日|きょう}は<b>{f|火曜日|かようび}</b>です。
Today is Tuesday.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|誕生日|たんじょうび}は<b>{f|四月|しがつ}{f|八日|ようか}</b>です。
My birthday is April 8th.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|電車|でんしゃ}は<b>{f|十五分|じゅうごふん}</b>{f|後|ご}に{f|来|き}ます。
The train comes in fifteen minutes.
</pre>

## Nuance & register

A clock time or a date takes に when something happens at it: {f|七時|しちじ}に{f|起|お}きる,
{f|五日|いつか}に{f|帰|かえ}る. Words that are already relative to today - {f|今日|きょう}, {f|明日|あした},
{f|毎日|まいにち}, {f|来週|らいしゅう} - take no に. With days of the week に is usual but may be left out in speech.

{f|一日|ついたち} is the first of the month; read いちにち, the same kanji means "one day" (a day long).
{f|二日|ふつか}, {f|三日|みっか} and the rest are also "two days", "three days" as lengths of time.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ よんじ → ✓ {f|四時|よじ}; ✗ きゅうじ → ✓ {f|九時|くじ}.
- ✗ にじゅうにち for the 20th → ✓ {f|二十日|はつか}.
- ✗ {f|明日|あした}に{f|行|い}きます → ✓ {f|明日|あした}{f|行|い}きます.

## Practice

**1.** How do you say 4 o'clock?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
{f|四時|よじ} - not よんじ and not しじ.
</details>

**2.** How do you say the first of the month?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
{f|一日|ついたち}. いちにち means "one day" as a length of time.
</details>

**3.** The exam is on June 20th: {f|試験|しけん}は{f|六月|ろくがつ}___です。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
はつか - the 20th has its own reading.
</details>

**4.** Read 六分 (six minutes).

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
ろっぷん - 1, 6, 8 and 10 double the consonant and take ぷん.
</details>
