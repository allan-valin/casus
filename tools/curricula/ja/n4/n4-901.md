# ～目 / ～番目

**Meaning** · ordinals - the second time, the third day, the fourth from the left

Japanese has no separate words for "first, second, third". You take a number with its counter and add
{f|目|め}: {f|二回|にかい} "twice" becomes {f|二回目|にかいめ} "the second time", {f|三日|みっか} "three days"
becomes {f|三日目|みっかめ} "the third day". When there is no natural counter, or the point is a position in a row
or a ranking, use {f|番目|ばんめ}.

## Formation

| Number + counter | + 目 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| {f|一回|いっかい} | {f|一回目|いっかいめ} | the first time |
| {f|二人|ふたり} | {f|二人目|ふたりめ} | the second person (child, guest…) |
| {f|三日|みっか} | {f|三日目|みっかめ} | the third day (of a stay, a course) |
| {f|二年|にねん} | {f|二年目|にねんめ} | the second year (at a job, of a marriage) |
| {f|二|ふた}つ | {f|二|ふた}つ{f|目|め} | the second one (corner, station, question) |
| {f|三番|さんばん} | {f|三番目|さんばんめ} | the third in a row or in order |

The result is a noun: {f|二回目|にかいめ}<b>の</b>{f|旅行|りょこう} "the second trip",
{f|三日目|みっかめ}<b>に</b> "on the third day", {f|二回目|にかいめ}<b>です</b> "it's the second time".

## Examples

<pre>
{f|日本|にほん}は<b>{f|二回目|にかいめ}</b>です。
This is my second time in Japan.
</pre>

<pre>
<b>{f|二|ふた}つ{f|目|め}</b>の{f|角|かど}を{f|右|みぎ}に{f|曲|ま}がってください。
Turn right at the second corner.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|旅行|りょこう}の<b>{f|三日目|みっかめ}</b>に{f|雨|あめ}が{f|降|ふ}りました。
It rained on the third day of the trip.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|右|みぎ}から<b>{f|三番目|さんばんめ}</b>の{f|人|ひと}が{f|田中|たなか}さんです。
The third person from the right is Mr Tanaka.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|会社|かいしゃ}で<b>{f|五年目|ごねんめ}</b>です。
This is my fifth year at this company.
</pre>

## Nuance & register

{f|目|め} turns an amount into a position, so the same number can mean two things:
{f|二日|ふつか} is "two days" or "the 2nd of the month", {f|二日目|ふつかめ} is only "the second day" of something
that started on day one.

Formal and written Japanese often puts {f|第|だい} in front instead: {f|第一回|だいいっかい} "the first
(meeting, edition)", {f|第二章|だいにしょう} "chapter two". In speech, {f|目|め} is the everyday way.

{f|一番|いちばん} alone means "the most, the best" (N5); "first in a row" is {f|一番目|いちばんめ}.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ {f|二|に}{f|目|め}の{f|駅|えき} → ✓ {f|二|ふた}つ{f|目|め}の{f|駅|えき}: {f|目|め} needs a counter between it and
  the number.
- ✗ {f|旅行|りょこう}の{f|三日|みっか}に → ✓ {f|旅行|りょこう}の{f|三日目|みっかめ}に. Without {f|目|め} it is the 3rd of
  the month.

## Practice

**1.** The second day of the course: {f|講座|こうざ}の{f|二日|ふつか}___

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
{f|目|め} - {f|二日目|ふつかめ}.
</details>

**2.** "My second child" - {f|二人|ふたり}___の{f|子|こ}ども

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
{f|目|め} - {f|二人目|ふたりめ}の{f|子|こ}ども.
</details>

**3.** "The fourth from the left" - {f|左|ひだり}から___

<details><summary>Answer</summary>
{f|四番目|よんばんめ} - a position in a row takes {f|番目|ばんめ}.
</details>
