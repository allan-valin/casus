# Conversation practice de · mk and de · ar — unit plan A1 to C2 (DRAFT for Allan, 2026-10-21, after his review)

Story bible: `tools/conversation/de/story-bible.yml` (Leipzig; Frau Becker, Jonas, Herr Schulz, Dr. Meier; later
Dr. Wagner, Lena, Herr Yilmaz, Frau Krüger, Prof. Hartmann at the Volkshochschule, Trainerin Petra at the
Sportverein). One German script, two narrator versions: the protagonist is a **nurse from Bitola** (Macedonian
narrator) or an **engineer from Aleppo** (Arabic narrator, Syria as the frame; other Arab countries come in through
the German characters' friends). Where the two paths differ (visa, recognition, religion, family) the unit has two
variants of the same scene. Written by AI (Claude); change anything. Many scenes come from *deutschweh* (2019, Schreibwerkstatt der Hochschule Fulda), a magazine of foreign students'
texts that Allan co-wrote: his own (the queue letter, safety, documents lost three times, classes all day, the club
cloakroom, money for the visa, minijobs) and the others' (umbrellas, silence, "is it hot for you too?", loneliness in
lectures, a refugee's first year, punctuality at 8:00, au pair). Used as story seeds, never quoted or named.

**Shape (Allan's review, 2026-10-21):** 30 units per level; A1–B2 in the rhythm L L T (daily life, daily life,
topic), the topics being the old C1/C2 themes made easier; C1–C2 all daily life with C-level grammar, the
Volkshochschule and the Sportverein as threads. 10–20 new items per unit, grammar spread over about two units.
**Reviewed** = what comes back on the doubling schedule. **No amounts** that change every year (Sperrkonto, BAföG,
fines, fees): units teach the rule and where to check it (Auswärtiges Amt, the Ausländerbehörde, the Studentenwerk).

Clash column: **mk** / **ar** = whose narrator gets the note; both = both.
