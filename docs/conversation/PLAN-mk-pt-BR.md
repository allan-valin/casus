# Conversation practice mk · pt-BR — unit plan A1 to C2 (DRAFT for Allan, 2026-10-21, after his review)

Story bible: `tools/conversation/mk/story-bible.yml` (Skopje, Карпош; Vera the landlady, Marko the neighbour, Ana the
colleague, Dragan at the corner shop; later Dr. Petrovska, Goran, Elena, Mr. Nikolovski, Prof. Ilievska, coach
Zoran). Written by AI (Claude); change anything. Units A1-01 and A1-02 are written and live.

**English version (Allan, 2026-10-21):** the same units with a neutral English narrator (for international offices): the
Brazilian comparisons are narrator steps marked `for: [pt]`, so the English version simply leaves them out and keeps
the curious note. The protagonist stays the story's Brazilian character (changing nationality would fork every dialogue).


**Shape, after Allan's review (2026-10-21):**
- 30 units per level. A1–B2: 20 **daily-life** units (L) and 10 **topic** units (T), in the rhythm L L T, so no two
  topic units come together. The topics are the former C1/C2 science and ideas themes, made easier at lower levels.
  C1–C2: 30 daily-life units that keep teaching grammar (the C-level Learn lessons), culture, the evening school
  and the basketball club.
- Pace of the classic audio courses (one of them, on your drive, reaches modal verbs and *weil* only at unit 30 of its first level): 10–20 new items per unit, and a
  Learn lesson spread over about two units instead of one per unit.
- **Reviewed** names what comes back and from where, on the doubling schedule (the previous unit, then within 2, 4,
  8 units); the checker enforces it item by item when the units are written. Every unit also gives about a quarter
  of its questions to review in a new setting.
- Amounts that change every year (prices, fees, fines) are never spoken as numbers: units teach the rule and where
  to check it.

Codes in the grammar column are the Learn lessons (`tools/curricula/mk/`).
