# Conversation practice en · de — unit plan (first level, 2026-10-28)

**Status: UNDER CONSTRUCTION / INCOMPLETE** — only the first level (A1) is planned; A2–C2 not planned yet.
English for German speakers; narrator German. Written by AI (Claude) after the guide (GUIDE.de.md).
Levels: CEFR is right for English (Cambridge, IELTS, the English Profile all use it).
Open: Learn's English lessons are written for Brazilian readers (pt-BR); German readers need the core grammar
lessons in German — a separate Learn task, not part of this course.

## Story
The protagonist (German, ~30, from Leipzig — f in odd units, m in even) moves to Boston for a two-year job in a
biotech lab. American English (US spelling, the US voice). Cast: Mrs. Rivera (landlady, 60s), Jamal (lab colleague,
32), Dr. Chen (the boss), Megan (neighbour, nurse, 28), Tony (runs the coffee shop on the corner).
German speakers' traps are taught on purpose: false friends (become ≠ bekommen, chef ≠ Chef), the th sounds,
w vs v, "I am here since…" (wrong), "make a photo" (wrong), do-questions and the -s of he/she/it.
