# Conversation practice ko · pt-BR — A1 unit plan (DRAFT for Allan, 2026-10-21)

Story bible: `tools/conversation/ko/story-bible.yml` (Seoul, 마포구; landlady 박순자 할머니, neighbour 민준, team lead
이지은 팀장님, the convenience-store 사장님). Written by AI (Claude); change anything. A2–C2 follow the bible's arc
and get their own plan after this one.

**Before units**
1. **Voice.** Piper ko_KR-kss, one female voice (allowed: non-commercial licence, Casus is not sold). Men: the same
   voice pitched down — I'll make samples for you to judge, as for Macedonian.
2. **Technical step.** The voice runs on our own browser engine with a hangul → sound converter (like the Macedonian
   one); built and tested before unit 1.

A1 grammar: 해요체 present (-아요/어요), 이에요/예요, 있어요/없어요, 주세요, -고 싶어요, particles 은/는, 이/가, 을/를,
에, 에서, both number systems, 안 negation, past -았/었어요 only as fixed phrases. Each unit: 15–30 new items, one
8–10-line dialogue, ~25 % review in a new setting. Protagonist f in odd units, m in even units (언니/오빠 vs 누나/형).
