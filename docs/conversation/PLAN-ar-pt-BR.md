Written by AI (Claude) after the guide (GUIDE.pt-BR.md); the Arabic awaits our proofreader, not a native check.

Levels: Arabic has no official exam of its own with level names; CEFR (A1…C2) is used by the main university

## Why MSA is believable in this story
The protagonist (Brazilian, ~30, grandchild of Lebanese immigrants in São Paulo — f in odd units, m in even) spends six
months at a university language centre in Amman. Teachers speak fuṣḥā in class; the classmates (Marcus from Germany,
Yuki from Japan, Elif from Turkey) share no other language, so they speak fuṣḥā to each other; the host family, the
Haddads, speak Jordanian Arabic among themselves and switch to fuṣḥā for the guest. A culture note says so in A1-03.

Script: every Arabic line fully vowelled (tashkīl) at this level — learners read it and the voice needs it.
Address by the unit's form: أنتَ / أنتِ, تفضّلْ / تفضّلي — both sets appear because the protagonist alternates.
