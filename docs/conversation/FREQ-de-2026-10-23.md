# German conversation — frequency warnings sorted (2026-10-23)

`node tools/conversation_freq.mjs de` reports 54 units in A1/A2 with more than three "rare" new items.
"Rare" means: not in `data/de/frequency.json`, which is only the **top 1,000 words of a general corpus**.
The A1/A2 vocabulary lists in Learn (1,298 words, Tatoeba frequency) don't help much either: only 18
of the 301 flagged words are in them. So the yardstick is too short, as it was for Japanese
(fixed there with the JLPT word list). Every flagged word, sorted by what should happen to it:

## What I'd do (one pass, all units, per the rule that a fix applies everywhere)
1. Replace the yardstick: a German top-5,000 list from an open frequency source (CC BY-SA, e.g. the
   Wiktionary/OpenSubtitles lists), as Japanese now uses JLPT N5+N4. Categories 1 and most of 3
   then stop being flagged automatically.
2. Count fixed phrases (category 2) as phrases in the checker.
3. Mark category 4 `rare: true` in the sources.
4. Apply only the few swaps in category 5 that keep the unit natural.
Expected result: 0 warnings, and no sentence made less natural to please the checker.
