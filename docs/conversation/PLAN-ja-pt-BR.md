Story bible: `tools/conversation/ja/story-bible.yml` (Hamamatsu, a large Brazilian community; the protagonist is a
nikkei grandchild who speaks no Japanese; landlady 鈴木さん, neighbour 健太, section chief 佐藤課長, the konbini 店長).
Same shape as the other plans: 30 units per level, L L T up to B2, C1–C2 daily life with grammar; 10–20 new items per
unit; a **Reviewed** column; no yearly amounts. A1 in full; A2–C2 as outlines until you confirm the shape.

**English version (Allan, 2026-10-21):** the same units with a neutral English narrator (for international offices): the
Brazilian comparisons are narrator steps marked `for: [pt]`, so the English version simply leaves them out and keeps
the curious note. The protagonist stays the story's Brazilian character (changing nationality would fork every dialogue).


**Before units:** the Japanese voice (Piper hi_fi_captain, a real female and male speaker) and a kana → sound
converter for our browser engine.

## A2–C2 outline (rows after you confirm the shape)

- **A2 — settling in:** residence card at city hall, bank, clinic, a 飲み会, the summer festival and yukata, onsen
  rules (tattoos), a first date and confessing (告白), a club night in Shizuoka, the grandmother's village; topics:
  water, electricity, money, geography, music, sayings.
- **B1 — friends:** opinions, nikkei identity (japonês in Brazil, ブラジル人 in Japan), a relationship, meeting the
  parents, a misunderstanding by indirectness, a wedding (御祝儀); topics: the cell, energy, statistics, happiness,
  freedom, art, literature, irony.
- **B2 — work:** 敬語 in earnest, meetings, e-mails, phone calls, interpreting for Brazilian workers; topics: genes,
  chemistry, the universe, probability, truth and media, politics with care, religion, **overwork, the ageing
  society and foreign workers** (social problems), the future.
- **C1 — life in depth:** contracts, a car (車検), a funeral, the school parents' association (PTA), mental health,
  raising a bilingual child, the evening course and a taiko club.
- **C2 — mastery:** speeches, irony, Hamamatsu dialect (遠州弁), negotiation, a lecture, staying or leaving.
