# Conversation practice de · mk and de · ar — A1 unit plan (DRAFT for Allan, 2026-10-21)

Story bible: `tools/conversation/de/story-bible.yml` — one German course, two narrators (Macedonian, Arabic), each with
its own protagonist background (a nurse from Bitola / an engineer from Aleppo) and its own culture notes, so the clash
scenes differ by narrator. The same German lines serve both; only the narrator text and some scenes change.

**Decisions for you**: (1) narrator voices — Macedonian as in the mk·pt-BR plan, Arabic with the browser's voice until an
open Arabic Piper voice is found (ar_JO-kareem states no licence); (2) one Arab country as the frame (Syria?) or a
general "Arab world"; (3) Leipzig as the city (East, cheaper, hospitals recruiting) or another.

Grammar follows Learn de A1 (present, sein/haben, articles and the accusative, modal verbs, Perfekt in the last units).
15–30 new items, one 8–10-line dialogue, ~25 % review in a new setting; protagonist f in odd units, m in even units.
