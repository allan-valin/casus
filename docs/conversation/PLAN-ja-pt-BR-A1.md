# Conversation practice ja · pt-BR — A1 unit plan (DRAFT for Allan, 2026-10-21)

Story bible: `tools/conversation/ja/story-bible.yml` (Hamamatsu, home of a large Brazilian community; the
protagonist is a nikkei grandchild who speaks no Japanese; landlady 鈴木さん, neighbour 健太, section chief 佐藤課長,
the konbini 店長). Written by AI (Claude); change anything. A2–C2 follow the bible's arc and get their own plan.

**Before units**
1. **Voice.** Piper ja_JP-hi_fi_captain has a real female and a real male voice (allowed: non-commercial licence).
2. **Technical step.** Its sounds are a Japanese set, not espeak: a kana → sound step in our browser engine; the
   units carry kana for every line. Built and tested before unit 1.

A1 grammar: です・ます present and past, 〜ません, は/が/を/に/で/へ, これ/それ/あれ, 〜をください, 〜たいです,
あります/います, numbers and counters (つ, 人, 枚), set 敬語 phrases. Each unit: 15–30 new items, one 8–10-line
dialogue, ~25 % review in a new setting. Protagonist f in odd units, m in even units (私 for both).
