## What the research found (2026-10-22)

- A2-10: Japanese surveys mostly answer "nationality doesn't matter, character does"; no reliable data that
  "most" women who like foreigners don't want a relationship — the plan now presents curiosity as real and
  says to make intentions clear, without the stereotype. International marriages ≈ 1 in 25; Japanese
  woman + foreign man ≈ a quarter of those. (kaken.nii.ac.jp; next-level.biz survey; ryugaku.kuraveil.jp)
- A2-16: 遠慮 — declining once and accepting the second offer is a widely described pattern in Japanese
  sources, but a custom, not a rule; sometimes the no is real. (haradaeigo.com/japanese-culture/w/enryo;
  chiebukuro discussions; gr.weworld.co.jp)
- Brazil: the service charge is optional by Lei 13.419/2017, customarily **10 %** (some places 12–15 %),
  not 20 %. (mpce.mp.br; exame.com; cnnbrasil.com.br)
- A2-17: since 2016 the Tourism Agency says a tattoo alone shouldn't be grounds to refuse; stickers,
  private baths 貸切風呂, rooms with their own bath. (travelvoice.jp 2016; honichi.com 2018; onsen.nifty.com)
- A2-24: "don't leave before the boss" — 82.8 % call it an outdated rule (2023 survey, kobe-np.co.jp);
  long-working bosses keep staff late (Persol study, president.jp). Paid leave 2024: 12.1 of 18.1 days,
  66.9 % (MHLW 就労条件総合調査 2025). Brazil CLT: 30 days férias + ⅓ bonus.
- B2-04: chopsticks upright = funeral rite; glass lower than a senior's at a toast — common manners advice,
  documented for Korea (ling-app.com), standard in Japanese business-manner teaching; escalators: Tokyo
  left / Osaka right, custom not law; Nagoya and Saitama ordinances ask people to stand still.
- ko B1-10: armistice 1953, no peace treaty; service 36 months at first, shortened step by step (army 18
  months today — check before writing the unit).
