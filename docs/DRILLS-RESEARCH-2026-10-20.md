# New drill types: research (2026-10-20)

Asked by Allan: "research in the sources for more kinds of drills exercises that could be added for everything".
What Casus has: endings tables (whole paradigm), letters game, frequent words and phrases (multiple choice), Learn
exercises (gap fill, choose the meaning, back-translation, word order). Missing: per-item production, parsing,
listening, spacing. Ranked by value for the effort; S/M/L = size.

1. **Spaced review queue** (S, all) — one "due today" session drawing items from every drill, FSRS/Leitner in the
   browser. Spacing (Cepeda et al. 2006, Psych. Bull., pubmed 16719566) and retrieval practice (Karpicke & Roediger
   2008, Science, pubmed 18276894).
2. **Parse the form** (S, inflecting languages) — show *puellārum*, pick case/number/gender (or tense/person/mood);
   every reading of a syncretic form accepted. The Wheelock/D'Ooge "identify" exercise; comprehension practice gives
   larger receptive gains (Shintani, Li & Ellis 2013, Language Learning 63(2)).
3. **One-step transformation** (S) — *amō* → plural / → perfect; type the neighbouring cell; interleaved across words
   (Nakata & Suzuki 2019, MLJ, doi 10.1111/modl.12581).
4. **Inflection cloze in real sentences** (M) — Tatoeba sentence, lemma given (*puella* →), type the form; production
   wins on delayed tests (Shintani et al. 2013).
5. **Who did what to whom** (M, case languages) — processing instruction: object-first sentences, choose the meaning
   or tap the subject (VanPatten & Cadierno 1993, SSLA 15(2)).
6. **Typed recall** (S, all) — gloss → type the word, accent-tolerant.
7. **Listening: choose the meaning** (S, TTS languages) — Tatoeba sentence played, no text.
8. **Minimal-pair listening** (M) — IPA edit distance 1 (Hölle/Hülle, long/short vowels); high-variability phonetic
   training (Thomson 2018, JSLP 4(2); Uchihara, Karas & Thomson 2025 meta-analysis, SSLA).
9. **Dictation** (S, TTS) — type a short sentence, word diff (Assimil; Davis & Rinvolucri, *Dictation*, CUP).
10. **Spot and fix the error** (M) — one word swapped for a wrong cell of its own paradigm; tap it, then fix it.
11. **Agreement builder** (M) — adjective (+ article) + noun into a given case and number.
12. **Sort into bins** (S) — gender, declension class, aspect pair, conjugation class.
13. **Shadowing / record and compare** (S–M, TTS) — Hamada 2016; Kadota 2019, RELC J.
14. **Sound ↔ spelling** (S, IPA) — IPA → word and word → IPA.
15. **Matching grid** (S, all) — 5×5 tap-to-pair warm-up.

Not automatable from current data: reading comprehension questions, sentence combining, composition, paragraph
ordering. Suggested order: 1, then 2, 3, 6 (paradigms already loaded), then one Tatoeba analysis index for 4, 5, 10,
11, then the TTS group (7, 8, 9, 13).

(Caveat from the research: Nakata & Suzuki and Hamada were read from abstracts and summaries only.)
