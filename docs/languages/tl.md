# Tagalog in Casus

Built 2026-10-21 on the generic builder (caseless), last in the order Allan accepted (hi, bn, id, vi, fa, sw,
**tl**). On Casus Asia.

- **Roles from the phrase markers**: ang / si (and ako, ikaw, siya…) the topic — what the verb's focus affix points
  at; ng / ni (and ko, mo, niya…) the non-topic actor or object; sa / kay (and akin, iyo…) the oblique: place, goal,
  indirect object. The colours follow the marker over the phrase, through the linker na / -ng
  (src/lang/tl/stages.js).
- **Verbs by aspect**: Wiktionary's completive (bumasa, nagbasa), progressive (bumabasa) and contemplative (babasa)
  are filed as past, present and future; the focus (actor -um- / mag-, object -in, locative -an, benefactive i-) is
  in the lemma (bumasa, basahin are separate entries).
- **Keys** drop the dictionary's stress and glottal marks (aklát, bumasâ); the linker -ng is read apart when the whole
  word is unknown (wikang → wika + -ng).
- Data: English Wiktionary via kaikki (CC BY-SA) → 35,299 lemmas, 53,121 forms. Baybayin spellings are left out.
- Voice: espeak-ng `tl`.
- Samples: everyday sentences written for Casus (1/59 unknown); *Sa Aking Mga Kabata* (attributed to Rizal, public
  domain), opening (1/28); Wikipedia's *Wikang Tagalog* (7/119: superlative pinaka-, reduplicated forms).
