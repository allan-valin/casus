# Indonesian in Casus

Built 2026-10-21 on the generic builder (caseless) and the shared role rules, next in the order Allan accepted
(hi, bn, **id**, vi, fa, sw, tl). On Casus Asia.

- **Roles, not cases**: subject – verb – object from word order; kepada / untuk / bagi + noun the indirect object.
  Pronouns and prepositions from a closed table.
- **Verbs** show voice, not person or tense: membaca (active, meN-), dibaca (passive, di-), baca (bare, imperative).
- **Clitics and reduplication**: -nya (his, her; the), -ku, -mu, -lah, -kah, -pun are read apart, and anak-anak as the
  plural of anak, when the whole word is unknown (src/lang/id/stages.js).
- Data: English Wiktionary via kaikki (CC BY-SA), 40,098 entries → 30,901 lemmas.
- Voice: espeak-ng `id`.
- Samples: everyday sentences written for Casus (0/58 unknown); the *Sumpah Pemuda* (1928, public domain) in today's
  spelling (2/34); Wikipedia's *Bahasa Indonesia* (7/113 — IPA and affixed derivatives such as dibakukan).
