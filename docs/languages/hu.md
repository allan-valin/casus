# Hungarian in Casus

Built overnight 2026-10-19 on the generic case-language builder.

- Eighteen cases on six hues: grammatical (nominative, accusative -t, dative -nak), inner (-ban in, -ból out of, -ba
  into), surface (-on on, -ról off/about, -ra onto), near (-nál at, -tól from, -hoz to); instrumental, causal-final,
  translative, terminative and the essives share the dative's hue, each with its own border and badge.
- Data: English Wiktionary via kaikki (CC BY-SA), streamed and kept for the commonest subtitle words.
- Not yet: the definite and indefinite conjugations told apart; many verb forms are missing from Wiktionary's tables.
