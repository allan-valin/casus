# Modern Greek in Casus

Built overnight 2026-10-19 on the generic case-language builder.

- Four cases (nominative, genitive, accusative, vocative); prepositions (σε, από, με, για…) take the accusative;
  σε + the article written together (στο, στην, στους…) splits into preposition and article; an article and its
  adjectives agree with the noun both ways; the vocative only where someone is addressed.
- Accents supplied from the dictionary when typed without them; Latin letters in the modern pronunciation
  (ELOT 743-style: β v, η i, αυ av/af, μπ b, ντ d). Alphabet drill with the modern names and sounds.
- Data: English Wiktionary via kaikki (CC BY-SA), kept for the 50,000 commonest subtitle words (11,300 lemmas).
- Not yet: names (Μαρία is unknown, so its article does not make it the subject); the future θα and the clitic
  pronouns as a chunk with their verb.
