# Macedonian in Casus

Built overnight 2026-10-19 (Allan's Zbor app is about Macedonian conversation; this is the reading side).

## What the reader does
- **The article is the colour.** Macedonian has no cases; its definite article is a suffix with three forms, and the
  block's colour says which: -от/-та/-то "the" (definite), -ов/-ва/-во "this" (near), -он/-на/-но "that" (far),
  no article (indefinite), and the vocative. Plural on a dashed border.
- **Verbs** carry person, number and tense (present, imperfect, aorist, imperative; the l-participle by gender), and the
  dictionary form names the aspect.
- A word with real readings drops the letter name it also spells and its interjection reading (е is "is").
- Latin letters as Wiktionary writes them (ѓ ǵ, ќ ḱ, ѕ dz, џ dž, љ lj, њ nj).

## Data
- English Wiktionary via kaikki (CC BY-SA): 41,700 lemmas, 537k forms; multiword perfects and form-of entries left out.

## Drills
- Cyrillic (31 letters, small and capital). Noun/adjective and verb tables with random words; frequent words
  (subtitles); phrases.
