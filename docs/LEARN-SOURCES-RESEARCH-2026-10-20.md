# Open sources for the Learn tab: research (2026-10-20)

Allan: "research again deeper to see if no free content is available … before you create lessons yourself".
Rule: only PD, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, GPL-family. Licences checked on the source page unless noted. No language has an
open graded inventory like JLPT/HSK; the usable pieces are open courses, PD textbooks/grammars and sentence data.

## Latin
- **Allen & Greenough, DCC edition** — ~640 numbered sections, HTML, CC BY-SA ("freely available for re-use under a
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license", dcc.dickinson.edu/node/27344). AVOID the Alpheios copy (BY-NC-SA).
- Gutenberg PD: Bennett, *New Latin Grammar* (#15665); Lane (#44653); D'Ooge (#18251, in use).
- Collar & Daniell, *First Year Latin* (1901), PD, graded lessons + exercises, IA scans (firstyearlatin00collrich).
- Wikibooks Latin (CC BY-SA, ~51 lessons). Tatoeba 56.7k.

## Ancient Greek
- **Ancient Greek for Everyone** (Major & Laughy 2018, Pressbooks), "CC-BY … 4.0 except where otherwise noted"
  (mds.marshall.edu/oa-textbooks/818) — check the "otherwise noted" parts. EPUB/XHTML export.
- Smyth (1920), Goodwin (1892): PD texts; AVOID Alpheios Smyth (BY-NC-SA). White, *First Greek Book* 1896 (PD, 80
  lessons; Greek OCR poor). First1KGreek (CC BY-SA, texts). AVOID Keller & Russell (Yale).

## Sanskrit
- **learnsanskrit.org guide** — ~100 graded lessons (script, sandhi, nominals, verbs, compounds), "This page is
  available under a CC BY 4.0 International license"; source on GitHub sanskrit/learnsanskrit.org (no LICENSE file;
  per-page statement). HIGH.
- Perry (1885), Macdonell (1927) PD, Devanagari OCR poor.

## Arabic (MSA)
- Wikibooks Arabic (CC BY-SA, 139 pages, few exercises). Thatcher 1911 + Key (PD, Halle copy under PDM). Wright (PD
  scans). Quranic Arabic Corpus (GPL, classical, morphology tags). AVOID Michigan State *Elementary Arabic* (BY-NC).

## Modern Greek
- **Wikibooks Modern Greek** (CC BY-SA, 173 pages, ~15 lessons, ~45 exercise pages). Thumb 1912 (PD, polytonic).
  AVOID Kallipos Poulopoulou (BY-NC-SA), greek-language.gr (©), Holton et al.

## German
- **Deutsch im Blick** (COERLL, CC BY 4.0, coerll.utexas.edu/dib) — 10 chapters A1–A2: dialogues, vocab, grammar,
  exercises. Boise State *Grammar to Accompany DiB* (CC BY 4.0 "except…"). Wikibooks German (~27 lessons). AVOID
  *Willkommen* (BY-NC-SA).

## Icelandic
- **DMII/BÍN** (CLARIN-IS, CC BY-SA 4.0) full inflection tables. UD Icelandic (CC BY-SA). No open modern course;
  AVOID Icelandic Online (©), Mímir (NC).

## Finnish
- Eliot, *A Finnish Grammar* 1890 (Gutenberg #59795, PD). FSI *Conversational Finnish* (US gov, PD, scans). Aalto
  OpenLearning *Finnish for Beginners* (CC BY 4.0 unless stated). UD FTB (CC BY; sentences from VISK). VISK's own
  licence NOT confirmed (Kotus varies; Dictionary of Modern Finnish is BY-ND) — confirm before quoting it.

## Hungarian
- **Ginever, *Hungarian Grammar* 1909** (Gutenberg #76725, PD, with exercises). FSI *Hungarian Basic Course* (PD,
  scans). AVOID UD Szeged (NC), *Syntax of Hungarian* (NC), MagyarOK (no licence).

## Turkish
- **Her Şey Bir Merhaba ile Başlar** (COERLL, CC BY-SA; intermediate–advanced, H5P exercises). UD Turkish GB (CC
  BY-SA; 2,880 example sentences by grammar topic, from Göksel & Kerslake — use sparingly). Wikibooks Turkish.
  FSI Turkish (PD). AVOID Yunus Emre (©), UD IMST (NC).

## Other (future languages)
- Dutch: **Wikibooks Dutch** (100% developed, 23 lessons + 16 practice). Swedish: DaLAJ (CC BY, learner error
  pairs). Norwegian: UD (CC BY-SA); NTNU course has no licence. Estonian: thin.
