# Japanese Learn — grammar audit (2026-10-27)

Method as for German and Macedonian (docs/learn/AUDIT-de-2026-10-27.md): a point counts as covered only when a lesson
explains it, anywhere in Japanese Learn.

## Inventories checked

- **OpenJLPT grammar lists** (vendor/openjlpt, 531 points N5–N1), matched by script against the 1,285 grammar lessons
  (JLPT Grammar Enhanced, 847 points, plus Wikibooks/Wikiversity pages); every point not matched by title was checked
  by hand in the lesson bodies (kanji/kana spellings: 際/さい, 限り/かぎり, 応えて/こたえて; points taught inside
  another lesson: purpose stem + に行く in n5-028, quotation と/って in n4-063/n4-064, づらい in n4-105).
- **System points** JLPT lists take for granted: verb groups and dictionary form (n5-021), て/た/ない-forms
  (n5-023/024/025), い/な-adjective inflection (n5-026/027), counters (n5-046), こ・そ・あ・ど (n5-013–015),
  transitive/intransitive pairs (n4-009), potential/passive/causative (n4-040–043), volitional (n4-082),
  conditionals (n4-048–050), imperative (n4-045), keigo (n4-112–120, n3-160).
