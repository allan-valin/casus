# German Learn audit (2026-10-27)

Allan asked: "Learn has no German numbers? … if numbers are missing, I wonder what else you forgot."

## What was actually wrong

- **My statement was wrong.** German Learn did have numbers: French Wikiversity "Nombres" quizzes (A1), the Spanish
  Wikibook's "Números" (A2) and the English Wikibook's "Numbers" (filed under B1 › Appendices). My coverage checker only
  looks at the main grammar path, and I reported "no lesson" without searching the rest of Learn.
- **The main grammar path had real gaps.** A1–B2 of that path is Grimm Grammar (UT Austin, CC BY 4.0), a grammar
  reference ordered by part of speech, not a course. Course basics with no part of speech of their own were never in
  it. The AI-written lessons (2026-10-20) only filled B2–C2; nobody had checked A1–B1 against a full syllabus.

## The check

The main path was compared point by point with the usual A1–B1 inventories (Goethe-Zertifikat A1–B1, Profile Deutsch).
A point counts as taught only when a lesson explains it; a word appearing in an example does not count.

| Level | Point | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | alphabet, spelling aloud, sounds | only in book chapters | de-a1-alphabet |
| A1 | numbers, prices, age, phone | only in book chapters (B1 appendix, A1 French quiz) | de-a1-numbers |
| A1 | es gibt | only in examples | de-a1-es-gibt |

| B1 | lassen (let, leave, have done) | only in examples | de-b1-lassen |

Points checked and found taught: personal pronouns, present tense and stem-changing verbs, sein/haben, questions, word
order, negation, articles, nominative/accusative/dative/genitive with their prepositions, two-way prepositions,
possessives, time, days, months, separable verbs, modals, imperative, Perfekt, Präteritum, Plusquamperfekt, future,
subordinating and coordinating conjunctions (weil, dass, ob, als, wenn, obwohl, nachdem…), adjective endings,
comparison, reflexive verbs, relative clauses, zu-infinitive and um…zu, passive, Konjunktiv II and I, n-declension,
verbs with prepositions and da-/wo-compounds. B2–C2 were written against the same inventories on 2026-10-20.

**Not covered by this audit:** pronunciation beyond the alphabet lesson (word stress, glottal stop), and the
placement question (modals and separable verbs sit at A2 here, A1 in Goethe's list). They are taught, just one level later.

## The new lessons

13 lessons in `tools/curricula/de/A1.yml`, `A2.yml`, `B1.yml`, marked AI-written on the page, in English and Portuguese,
with Macedonian and Arabic in `i18n/*.A1-B1.yml`. Each lesson has 100–200 Tatoeba exercises. Every German word in
them was checked against the dictionary (0 unknown words).

They were added with `tools/learn_add_written.py` instead of a full rebuild. Rebuilding German Learn from scratch no
longer reproduces the published data: the Arabic machine-translation cache was renamed (`cache.ar.eurollm.json`), and
three Wikibooks pages (de-wb-112–114, among them the Numbers appendix) are missing from `vendor/`. A full rebuild would
have dropped those, so the published data was kept and the new lessons spliced in. The published copy was compared
with the local one before uploading: they were identical apart from the additions.
